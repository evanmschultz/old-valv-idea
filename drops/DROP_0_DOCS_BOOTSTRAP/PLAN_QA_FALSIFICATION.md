# DROP_0 — PLAN QA FALSIFICATION (Round 5)

**Verdict:** FAIL

Round 5's accepted finding F1-R4 fix — switching the Charm sub-check from `grep -c 'charm.land/' <file>` (line-count) to `grep -o 'charm.land/' <file> | wc -l` (occurrence-count), floor 3 retained — correctly removes the single-line-collapse false-fail the prior form had, but the cure introduces a worse disease: **occurrence-count over a bare-phrase pattern no longer guards against the silent-deletion attack the floor exists to catch.** Item 37's explicit claim — *"Floor of 3 retained (still guards against silent deletion of more than one of the four Charm direct imports)"* — is false under a trivial counterexample. A second, weaker defect is that `charm.land/` has an unescaped regex `.` metacharacter, so `charm/land/` and similar near-miss strings also match.

## Findings

| ID | Severity | Unit/Item | Counterexample | Mitigation |

(empirically `/usr/bin/grep -o 'charm.land/' /tmp/charm_minimal.md \| /usr/bin/wc -l` → `3` for prose "The charm.land/ ecosystem ships charm.land/ bubbles and charm.land/ ghostty."). Either fix would be a one-character edit and does not interact with the F1-R5 fix above (the import-shape or per-import alternatives also resolve this implicitly). |

## Attacks Attempted And Refuted

1. **Sibling floor-1 single-line collapse (attack 3).** Claim in item 37: the floor-1 sibling checks (`modernc.org/sqlite`, `testcontainers`, `modelcontextprotocol/go-sdk`) are not vulnerable to single-line-collapse because `grep -c` and `grep -o … | wc -l` agree on `>= 1`. Verified empirically: `printf 'modernc.org/sqlite ... modernc.org/sqlite ... modernc.org/sqlite' | grep -c 'modernc.org/sqlite'` → `1`, `grep -o ... | wc -l` → `3`; both satisfy floor-1. Claim holds. REFUTED as attack.

2. **BSD grep `-o` portability (attack 4).** `man grep` on macOS shows `-o, --only-matching` is supported. No darwin-version gotcha — `-o` has been in BSD grep for many macOS releases. Item 37's "BSD/macOS-portable" claim for `-o` is correct. REFUTED as attack.

3. **Polarity drift in new line 55 / item 37 (attack 5).** Checked whether the new parenthetical prose in line 55 or item 37 itself contains any phrase that collides with U0.2 crit 11(b)'s `no cycles|strictly layered` or crit 13(c)'s `authoritative|defers to|source of truth` polarity windows. Neither new text contains those phrases. Additionally, those polarity checks operate on `main/CLAUDE.md` (the builder's target file), not on the drop's `PLAN.md`, so PLAN.md-internal polarity phrases are irrelevant to the checks regardless. REFUTED as attack.

4. **Criterion count drift (attack 6).** Enumerated U0.2 criteria via `awk '/^### Unit 0\.2/,/^### Unit 0\.3/' PLAN.md | grep -E '^  [0-9]+\.'`. Criteria 1 through 15 present, exactly 15 items. Round 5's item 37 lives in a prose "Round 5 revisions" block appended after the units section — it is numbered as a revision-log entry, not a criterion, and cannot be miscounted as a 16th U0.2 criterion because the extractor regex anchors to the unit subsection. REFUTED as attack.

5. **Round-4-content stability (attack 7).** `git diff HEAD~1 HEAD -- drops/DROP_0_DOCS_BOOTSTRAP/PLAN.md --stat` → 5 insertions, 1 deletion. Full diff shows exactly two hunks: (a) the line-55 replacement of the Charm sub-check form, and (b) the appended Round 5 revisions header + item 37. No prior-round item was silently edited. The claim of Round-5-scope-minimality holds. REFUTED as attack.

## Verdict

FAIL — one blocking finding (F1-R5) and one concern (F2-R5). F1-R5 invalidates item 37's core claim that the occurrence-count floor "still guards against silent deletion of more than one of the four Charm direct imports" — it does not, because `grep -o 'charm.land/'` counts phrase occurrences, not distinct imports, so a builder can delete every Charm import and satisfy the floor by writing three bare `charm.land/` mentions in prose. Round 4's F1 was a false-fail (legitimate intent rejected by the check); Round 5's fix replaces it with a false-pass (illegitimate intent accepted by the check), which is worse for a silent-deletion-guard criterion. The recommended remediation is per-import floor-1 checks (mirroring the splitting-per-category rationale already applied to SQLite / testcontainers / MCP-SDK in crit 9) or an import-shape regex piped through `sort -u`. F2-R5 (unescaped regex dot) is a cosmetic/defensive tightening that should be applied at the same time as the F1-R5 fix, since any of the recommended remediations naturally resolves it too.
